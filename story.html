Our story

Never met a stranger

Petronita was a woman who never met a stranger.

She had an extraordinary way of making people feel seen, valued and welcomed, especially those society often overlooked. She devoted her time to caring for the homeless and the less fortunate, personally transporting individuals to food banks, shelters and local assistance programs when others turned away.

A restaurant is an odd sort of memorial. But it is the right one here, because what she did was feed people and make room for them, and that is the whole job. The name over the door is a standard to keep, not a decoration.

So the room is run to a simple rule. Thoughtfully crafted cuisine, with warm, welcoming hospitality. Everyone who sits down gets the same five courses and the same attention, whether it is a first date, a fiftieth birthday, or somebody eating alone at the end of a hard week.

A nocturnal tribute to soul, spice and silver smoke, served under candlelight.

The cooking

Two traditions that were already related

Southern and Latin food are put side by side here because they already share a pantry: corn, pork, citrus, chili, slow heat and long patience.

One

One menu a night

Dinner is a single five-course tasting menu. Nobody is choosing between things the kitchen half-wants to cook. Everything on it is there because it earned the place.

Two

It changes with the season

The menu is written for a month or two at a time. Watermelon in late summer, blood orange when there is blood orange. When the season turns, so does the menu.

Three

Nobody eats a side dish

The vegan tasting menu is not the same plate with the meat removed. It is five courses of its own, built from the start, and it goes out at the same moment as everything else.

The neighbourhood

Historic Springfield

1349 N. Market Street sits in Springfield, the old brick neighbourhood just north of downtown Jacksonville. It is one of the city's original districts, and it has spent the last few years filling back up with the kind of small, particular businesses it was built for.

The bar list nods to it directly. There is a Springfield Fizz and a Historic District Flight on the cocktail menu, and a Garden District Mimosa at brunch.
