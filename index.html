Our story

The woman the room is named for

Petronita was a woman who never met a stranger.

She had an extraordinary way of making people feel seen, valued and welcomed, especially those society often overlooked. She devoted her time to caring for the homeless and the less fortunate, personally driving people to food banks, shelters and local assistance programs when others turned away.

The restaurant carries her name because it is run the same way. Thoughtfully crafted cuisine, and hospitality that treats a first-time guest like someone already known.
